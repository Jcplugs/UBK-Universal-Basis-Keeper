# Inside the cover

**Every item has a price. Your inventory has a story.**

There is a useful habit to develop at the auction house: before asking what an item might sell for, ask how it arrived in your bags.

Perhaps you bought the ore in three lots at three prices. Perhaps the gem came from a prospecting session. Perhaps that stack has been sitting in the bank long enough that the market remembers something different from what you paid.

The price on the board is worth knowing. So is the bill in your ledger.

Universal Basis Keeper keeps those records within reach of your TradeSkillMaster routine. Open Cost Coverage to see what is known and what still needs an explanation. Bring a recipe to Market Inspector and compare its material costs with current shopping quotes. Review an ore before prospecting it. Choose a few items to Sell Above Basis, then put their group assignments back when the session is over.

There is no need to turn every price movement into a prediction. A useful tool should show its working, admit a missing input, and let you make the decision.

UBK's figures are part of that working. A basis tells you about an investment. A yield estimate describes observed or imported results. A market quote describes a moment. None of them, alone, is a buyer.

Welcome to the workshop. Start with something familiar, check the figures, and keep the record as you go.
