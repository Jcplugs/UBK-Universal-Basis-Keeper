UBK / GOBLIN INTELLIGENCE v1.1.0
CHANGELOG
=================================

v1.1.0 is a major feature and usability release built on the original public RC foundation. The original release already included UBK acquisition-cost tracking for TSM crafting materials, safe TSM onboarding/review, Radar, Market Inspector, Watchlist, Cost Review, and Cost Coverage. The changes below describe what has been added or substantially improved since that baseline.

RELEASE VERSIONING
------------------
- Release versioning standardized: beginning with v1.1.0, UBK / Goblin Intelligence uses a simplified sequential version and filename format for future releases.

BROADER UBK INVENTORY COVERAGE
------------------------------
- UBK is no longer limited to items native to TSM's crafting-material list.
- Purchased resale stock, reputation items, investment inventory, keys, gear, and other purchased items can carry a real UBK acquisition basis without being forced into TSM's crafting-material system.
- UBK distinguishes known-cost units from unresolved units instead of silently treating uncertain inventory as free.
- Personal UBK basis information can appear directly in normal item tooltips.
- Existing trusted basis remains useful even when an item is temporarily out of stock.

LOOT / FARMED INVENTORY HANDLING
--------------------------------
- UBK now watches actual loot-window activity instead of treating every unexplained inventory increase as a purchase or free acquisition.
- Looted/farmed inventory is kept distinct from purchase evidence.
- First-seen loot with no defensible starting basis is routed to human review rather than assigned an invented cost.
- Established tracked items can absorb supported loot/farmed units while preserving the distinction in provenance and detail views.

SHREDDER
--------
- Goblin Intelligence now includes a first-class Shredder workspace for disenchant-based gold making.
- Shredder can build a cached candidate list from items known through TSM/UBK and surface opportunities for player review.
- Candidate rows provide an obvious Search TSM action for live Auction House confirmation.
- Market Inspector can show expected disenchant-output context for eligible TBC items.
- Shredder remains decision support only: it never buys or disenchants automatically.

OWNED SHREDDER PIPELINE
-----------------------
- Shredder can follow disenchantable gear already owned by the player.
- Owned items can be organized into practical states such as READY, NEEDS MAIL, IN TRANSIT, STORED, and LISTED.
- The designated Shredder character is configurable by the user.
- Cross-character mail is treated as owned inventory movement rather than a sale followed by a free acquisition.

DISENCHANT TRANSFORMATION ACCOUNTING
------------------------------------
- When a tracked item is actually disenchanted, UBK can transfer the source item's supported acquisition cost into the actual enchanting materials produced.
- Shred History records the source item, actual output, and cost-transfer context.
- If the source item's cost is unresolved, UBK keeps the resulting uncertainty instead of inventing a clean-looking basis.

COST COVERAGE IS NOW AN ACTIVE WORKSPACE
----------------------------------------
- Cost Coverage category counts are clickable filters: ALL, READY, PARTIAL / SAFE, UNKNOWN COST, REVIEW, FORMULA-PROTECTED, and ZERO-STOCK.
- Hover help explains that clicking a category filters the list below.
- PARTIAL / SAFE rows now include an obvious Resolve Unknown action.
- Resolve Unknown opens a simple item-specific popup showing the item icon, tooltip, known/owned/unresolved counts, and an explanation of what PARTIAL / SAFE means.
- The user enters a per-unit cost only for the unresolved quantity; already-known units are not rebased.
- Resolution uses a two-stage Review -> Confirm Resolve workflow.
- Before commit, the confirmation shows the unresolved quantity, entered price, current basis, and projected new blended basis.
- The projection is recalculated immediately before commit. If the underlying inventory/basis changed while the dialog was open, the stale confirmation is rejected and the user must review the updated numbers.
- Coverage refreshes after a successful resolution so items visibly move out of PARTIAL / SAFE when they become READY.

GOBLIN INTELLIGENCE — EASIER ACCESS
-----------------------------------
- New UBK / GI Home landing page provides one consistent starting point for the addon suite.
- Home shows a quick health summary and direct links into the main UBK and GI workflows.
- Standalone GI and UBK minimap launchers are available without opening the Auction House or a profession window.
- Left-click and right-click use the same simple behavior: open Home.
- The minimap tooltip simply explains that clicking opens the UBK / GI interface.
- /gi with no argument opens the same Home page.
- The old GI launcher on the profession/TSM window has been removed.

GOBLIN INTELLIGENCE — WINDOW / UI IMPROVEMENTS
-----------------------------------------------
- Escape now closes Goblin Intelligence like a normal WoW interface window.
- Shift + drag resizes the GI window.
- Pagination adapts to smaller window heights instead of drawing rows off-screen.
- Item-driven GI views now use real TBC item icons where the client supplies them.
- Hovering item icons provides the normal item tooltip.
- The interface has been rebuilt toward a more WoW-native Goblin Workshop presentation with brass/gold framing, a goblin emblem, clearer navigation, and the fortune charm along the bottom.
- The visual treatment is decorative only; actual item/market content comes from TBC Classic, UBK, and TSM data.

GI RELIABILITY / WORKFLOW HARDENING
-----------------------------------
- Cost Coverage handling was hardened for empty or incomplete categories.
- Pagination behavior across list pages was repaired and made reusable.
- Shredder's candidate scan was reworked to avoid relying on inaccessible TSM internals.
- Candidate scanning can build incrementally from information available to companion addons.
- Expected disenchant-output handling was made more resilient for the same reason.
- Live confirmation remains intentionally in the player's hands.

PUBLIC-DISTRIBUTION SAFETY
--------------------------
- No SavedVariables are included.
- No donor/player purchase archive is included.
- No preset inventory, item bases, watchlists, realm/faction state, character lists, groups, operations, or personal market history are included.
- The Shredder character is selected by the recipient.
- Existing TSM users receive an installer that does not replace TSM/AppHelper.
- A separate fresh-install path includes the validated TSM core and a sanitized AppHelper shell with no donor market data.

CORE SAFETY PRINCIPLES RETAINED
-------------------------------
- Unknown cost remains unknown until supported by evidence or explicitly reviewed.
- UBK owns acquisition accounting; GI presents workflows and market context.
- GI remains player-controlled and advisory.
- UBK/GI do not automatically buy, bid, mail, disenchant, post, cancel, or craft.
